# Prompt Anatomy Ecosystem

**Prompt Anatomy is an AI Operating System for modern teams.**

6-module interactive training (6-block methodology) plus an ecosystem of focused prompt kits — from a free first lesson through executive playbooks.

The ecosystem is built around one simple idea:

**AI should not be used only as a chat tool. It should become a structured work system.**

---

## 1. Main Platform

### **promptanatomy.app**

**6-module training hub for teams and leaders.**

The core hub ships interactive training on the 6-block prompt methodology (META → INPUT → OUTPUT → REASONING → QUALITY → ADVANCED), plus checkout and lifetime access. Plans are Starter (modules 1–3) and Core (full 1–6); Team Pilot is for cohort rollout. The interactive app lives at /anatomy/. Do not publish EUR amounts on the marketing site.

It includes:

* **Starter (modules 1–3) and Core (full 1–6)**
* **6-block methodology: META → INPUT → OUTPUT → REASONING → QUALITY → ADVANCED**
* **500+ prompt library in the training app**
* **Certificate on passing score; lifetime access**
* **Team Pilot for cohort rollout**

Aggregate ecosystem counts (600+ templates, 60 tools, 100-term glossary) span training plus subdomain kits — not one library on a single URL.

**Best for:** CEOs, COOs, IT leaders, managers, and teams that want structured AI training.

## 2. Enter

### **promptanatomy.cloud**

**Start with the first free lesson.**

A 15-slide interactive lesson (EN and LT): five-part enter frame (Role, Context, Reasoning, Output, Quality control), Quick Send Check, copy-ready library, and PDF summary. No account and no paid tier on this URL.

**Best for:** all staff, beginners, and teams that need a simple first step into AI.
